the agency wrangler.

The job: be the day-to-day contact for local agencies.

Agencies do their best work when there's one thread, one deadline per ask, and decisions written down. This is the Monday email I'd send to the social, influencer and PR agencies, plus how I'd run the weekly call.

To: Social agency, Influencer agency, PR agency
Cc: Kim
Subject: WGAC Canada · WIP · week of Nov 23

Hi all,

Happy Monday. Here's the week in one place. Reply on this thread so nothing gets lost.

Decisions we need from you

  • Influencer: final usage terms for the two open creators by Tue 12pm, so Kim can sign off Wednesday.
  • PR: which 3 outlets are most likely to run the Gift Edition in a holiday gift guide. We'll send product there first.

Due this week

  • Social: Giving Tuesday Reel, first cut by Wed 5pm. Global brand team sees it Thursday.
  • Influencer: creator briefs out by Wed, product shipped by Thu (Ontario creators get it in 2 to 3 business days).
  • PR: boreal fact sheet updated with the latest donation total.

Heads up

  • We don't ship to Quebec yet. Please no "across Canada" in any copy, and no Quebec-based creators or giveaways for now.
  • Free shipping over $40 is for Ontario, Alberta and BC only. Say it that way, or leave it out.

Thanks, and shout if anything's slipping. I'd rather hear on Tuesday than on Friday.

Durva

Thursday agency call · 30 min

  1. 5 min: last week's numbers (one slide each, no decks)
  2. 10 min: what's at risk and what we need from each other
  3. 10 min: next week's content against the global calendar

How I'd work with agencies

  • One shared tracker. Agencies update their own rows.

+

small things i noticed.

From ca.whogivesacrap.org, checked Nov 14, 2026. All small, all easy wins.
  • Garbage or trash? The Canadian homepage uses both words (and "Trash, compost and pet poo!"). Canadians mostly say garbage bags and search for them that way. Picking one helps search and feels more local.
  • A home for business enquiries. The AU footer links to Wholesale, a Discount program and an Affiliate program. The Canadian site doesn't have these yet, so a one-page "Who Gives A Crap for business" in Canada would give bulk and office enquiries somewhere to land (copy for it is in roll 07).
  • Free shipping is regional. The shipping page is clear that free shipping over $40 is for Ontario, Alberta and BC. The homepage says "most orders over $40", which is true, and a province note there would save some confused carts in Manitoba and Nova Scotia.
