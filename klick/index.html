So what
  • Search CPCs rose 12% after a competitor launch. Visits are slightly under plan.
  • CTV completion rate is 94%, the best line in the plan.
  • Mobile in-app is under-pacing at 60%: inventory is thin on the approved app list.
  • Recommendation: move $20K from mobile to search for weeks 7 to 9, and add two approved apps. Planner to approve.
07 · AI in the workflow

AI for the first pass. People for the sign-off.

Three prompts that save time on pacing, reporting and QA.

Pacing check

Spot problems on Monday morning

Here is this week's pacing export.
Flag any line outside 90-110% of
expected spend, explain the likely
cause in one line each, and suggest
a reallocation that keeps the total
budget the same.
Report draft

Turn data into a first draft

Summarize week 6 for a media
planner in 4 bullets: what moved,
why, what it means, what we should
do next. Use only the numbers in
the table. No new figures.
QA

Catch naming mistakes

Check these 60 placement names
against our naming convention.
List every name that breaks a rule
and show the corrected version.
My rule: no patient data, unapproved claims or client-confidential plans go into public AI tools. Only approved, company-sanctioned tools, and a human checks every output.
