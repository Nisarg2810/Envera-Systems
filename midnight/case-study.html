Why it matters

The cheapest day
is the one you don't need.

A lower day rate is a discount. Fewer days is a saving. On Crestline's well, keeping the curve and the lateral in one run removed the trips that usually eat days on this interval.

The ask

Drill the curve and a lateral of about 10,500 ft for Crestline, on an interval where a trip between the two is normal.

What we did

Drilled the curve and the full lateral with one bottom hole assembly in a single run. No trips.

The result

Average lateral ROP of 92 ft/hr, about 15% faster than the offset wells, and the well finished 4.5 days ahead of plan.
