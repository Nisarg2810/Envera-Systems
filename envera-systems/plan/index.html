Honest about the hard parts

What could slow us down, and what I'd do about it.

If I'm new to security…
I learn it at the source: a central-station shift, sales calls and a 20-question FAQ bank. Ops checks every technical claim before it's published.
If approvals slow things down…
Batch approvals once a week, a two-week buffer in the calendar, and templates that need fewer edits.
If there's too much to do…
We agree the top three priorities each month. Everything else goes in a visible backlog, not in your inbox.
If the HubSpot data is messy…
Clean-up comes before automation. No nurture goes live on lists we can't trust.
If customer stories are hard to get…
Ask customer success for five happy communities in week 1, and use a simple consent and interview kit.
If a channel isn't working…
The monthly scorecard says so early, and we move the effort to what is.
