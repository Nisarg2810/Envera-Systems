Prep sheet (5 minutes)

  • Firm, province, size of book, who they serve (young families? retirees?)
  • How they found us: inbound form, webinar, referral
  • Ready to send: the one-pager and The Inheritance Gap Report
  • Goal for the call: one client invite sent this week
Calendar invite note sample

Hi Daniel, looking forward to Thursday! In 20 minutes we'll show you the dashboard, how a client invite works, and what your clients see. Bring any client situation you're unsure about.

Durva, Willful

Discovery questions

  1. How do estate planning conversations come up with your clients today?
  2. What happens when a client says "I'll get to my will later"?
  3. Which clients would you invite first?
  4. Do you work with your clients' adult children yet?
  5. What would make this an easy yes for your practice?

Then: show the dashboard and send one test invite to their own email, live, so they see exactly what a client gets.

"Are online wills actually legal?"Yes. A Willful will is legal in Canada once it's signed and witnessed properly, and every will comes with clear signing instructions.
"Is my clients' data safe?"Willful uses strong encryption, runs regular audits, and works with brands like Scotiabank, CIBC and Wealthsimple. Documents aren't stored on Willful's servers.
"What if a client's estate is complicated?"Around 80 to 90% of Canadian estates likely don't need a lawyer. For trusts, business succession or assets overseas, we'd point them to an estate lawyer.
"Is this going to replace me?"No. It gives you a reason to have the conversation, and keeps you in the middle of it.

07
Fresh activations

Five partner ideas I'd bring to the table

The job post: "Bring fresh, practical creative ideas to the table for new partner activations."
Moment-based

The beneficiary moment

When a customer names a beneficiary on a TFSA or RRSP, show a gentle "what about everything else?" message with the Willful offer.

Practical because the partner already owns the moment. We just add one message.

Seasonal

Do your taxes, do your will

A March and April push with tax-filing partners. Same mindset, same sitting: finally getting the grown-up paperwork done.

Practical because tax season already has people's attention.

Life event

New home, new will

Mortgage partners add the offer to the "keys in hand" email. A home is often the biggest thing someone owns, and that's when they think about protecting it.

Practical because it's one line in an email the partner already sends.

Advisors

The next-generation invite

Advisors invite their clients' adult children to make a will. It builds a relationship with the family before the money moves.

Practical because it's exactly the gap The Inheritance Gap Report is about.

Workplace

Benefits enrolment season

Offer Willful to group benefits providers as a perk employees can add during enrolment. Start with one provider and one pilot employer, measure completions, then grow it.
