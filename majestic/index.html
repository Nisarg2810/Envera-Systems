Video

Three Reels to film on site.

Reels are where your account already shines. These are short scripts with a shot list, so filming takes one site visit.

Reel 1 · 20 seconds

Day 1 vs The Big Day

  1. Excavator, muddy yard, text: "Day 1."
  2. Quick cuts: forms, liner going in, coping, patio.
  3. Same angle, finished pool at twilight, text: "The Big Day."
  4. End card: "Your yard will be a mess. That's ok." + 705-623-8048.

Audio: a trending "transformation" sound. Hook in the first second.

Reel 2 · 30 seconds

60 seconds with Ryan

  1. Ryan arriving at 7 AM, coffee, text: "First to arrive."
  2. Three quick answers: favourite part of a build, the one thing homeowners never expect, best tip for new pool owners.
  3. End: "18 years at Majestic. Last to leave."

Shot on a phone, no script needed. People trust people.

Reel 3 · 15 seconds

Twilight switch

  1. Mills Circle at golden hour, locked-off shot.
  2. Lights click on: pool, landscape lighting, fire pit.
  3. Text: "Same backyard. Different mood."

Pairs with the Twilights highlight and post 2.

Home shows and events

Before, during and after.

A home show is three weeks of content, not one weekend. This is how I'd run the social side.

2 weeks before

Fill the booth

  • "Come see us at booth #" post with a project photo and dates
  • Countdown story with a reminder sticker
  • "Ask Marette" teaser: bring your backyard photos for a free design chat
  • DM past enquiries who never booked
At the show

Capture everything

  • Booth setup timelapse for Reels
  • Stories through the day: visitors, Chris and Marette, sketches on the spot
  • A QR code on the booth to book a design consult
  • Short interviews: "What's on your backyard wish list?"
The week after

Turn visitors into quotes

  • Thank-you post with the best photo
  • Follow-up email to every visitor within 48 hours
  • "Most asked questions at the show" carousel
  • Track consults booked from the show
