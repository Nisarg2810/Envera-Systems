Safety + permission

On site, the right way

  • Hard hat, vest and boots on every site visit
  • Check in with Ryan before filming; stay out of active work zones
  • Written OK from homeowners before posting their yard or family
  • No house numbers, plates or faces without consent
Editing short-form

One shoot, five pieces

  • 1 Reel (15 to 30 s, captions on)
  • 1 carousel (cover + 3 details)
  • 3 Stories (poll, question, link)
  • TikTok + YouTube Shorts from the same Reel
  • Facebook cross-post with a longer caption

Tools: CapCut presets in Majestic fonts and colours, Canva brand kit, Lightroom for twilight shots.
