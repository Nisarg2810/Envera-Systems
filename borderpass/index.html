Study permit refused: what to do in the next 30 days

Target: borderpass.ai/blog · Converts to: /visa-refusal · 1,600 to 1,900 words

Who is searching

Someone holding a refusal letter, scared, on their phone at night. They want to know: is it over, what are my options, how fast do I need to act?

Answer box (first 60 words, for Google and AI answers)
A study permit refusal is not permanent. Start by reading the reasons in your letter and asking for the officer's notes. Then choose one of three paths: reapply with a stronger file, ask for reconsideration, or seek judicial review. Judicial review has short deadlines, so get advice early.
Outline

  4. Step 3: Reapply, reconsideration or judicial review (comparison table)
  5. Step 4: Rebuild your file: funds, study plan, ties
  6. Step 5: When to bring in a lawyer
  7. FAQ (with FAQ schema)
