Account-based marketing

The ABM program, in full.

Who we go after, how we know an account is warming up, what we do at each stage, and who does it. Built with the MDR, reviewed every two weeks.

1 · Who: the ideal account and its buying group

Ideal account

  • Industry: banks, insurers, health systems, pharma, regulated tech
  • Size: 1,000+ employees, North America first (Toronto + NYC teams)
  • Trigger: an active AI program on sensitive data (RAG, agents, model training)
  • Pain: lots of unstructured data with PII/PHI: calls, notes, claims, chats
  • Not now: existing customers (RBC, Zurich, Intact, MUFG…) go to customer marketing, not ABM

04
Events

Make people come to the booth before the event starts.

Inspired by how Exit Five sponsors win events: show up for months before, speak without pitching, run ads to attendees with no offer, and give people something to talk about. Here's the version for privacy and security events.

LESSON 1Be known before you arriveMonths of podcast spots, newsletter mentions and helpful posts in privacy communities, so buyers say "I've seen you everywhere."
LESSON 2Speak, don't pitchA talk on "where PII leaks in RAG pipelines" with zero product slides. The booth gets the traffic.
LESSON 3"See you at IAPP"LinkedIn ads to attendees before the event. No offer, no button. Just a friendly hello they'll remember.
LESSON 4A demo with an "aha"A 90-second booth demo per persona: paste a messy document, watch it redact live, inside the VPC.
