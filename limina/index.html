  • What were you trying to do with this data?
  • What did you try before? What broke?
  • Why Limina? What did the evaluation look like?
  • What changed, in numbers? Time, accuracy, cost

Objection cards

From Limina's own site
“We already use AWS Comprehend / Presidio.”Pattern matching misses context: an SSN without the word “SSN”, order numbers flagged as cards. Offer a side-by-side on their data.
“Our data can't leave our environment.”It doesn't. Limina runs in their VPC or on-prem, with no third-party access and no outbound calls.
“We built our own scripts.”Text, then JSON, then audio, then a new language: each one a new edge case to maintain. 52 languages and 50+ entity types out of the box.
