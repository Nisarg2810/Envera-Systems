09 · POs, invoices, expenses

Every invoice matched to a PO before anyone pays it

A simple three-way check: the PO, the invoice and proof the work was delivered. If any of the three is missing or the amounts differ, the row stops.

“Assist with purchase requests, purchase orders, invoice processing, and expense reconciliation.”
POVendorForPO amountInvoiceInvoicedReceivedCheck

Purchase request

What, why, which budget line, amount with tax and the vendor quote attached. It goes to the budget owner before a PO is raised.

Month-end reconcile

Card expenses and invoices matched to budget lines by the 3rd working day. Anything without a receipt gets one reminder, then goes to the Team Lead.

Open commitments

POs raised but not yet invoiced are counted as spent in the tracker, so the budget never looks better than it really is.

10 · Proof of execution

A co-op claim that gets paid the first time

A POE pack is easy to build while the campaign runs and painful to rebuild afterwards. The folder opens on launch day and fills up as things happen.

“Coordinate proof-of-execution (POE) reports for campaigns, events, and partner programs … and supporting claim and reimbursement submissions.”

The POE report, one page per activity

What goes to the partner or the regional team
  1. Activity: what ran, where, and on which dates
  2. Proof: dated screenshots and photos, one per placement
  3. Money: agreed amount, invoice and PO number
  4. Results: sell-through in the window against the 4 weeks before
  5. Sign-off: the channel lead’s name and date

File name builder

Three rules

  • Only _CURRENT is shared. When a file is replaced, the old one moves to _ARCHIVE the same day.
  • Every English file has a French twin or a note saying why not.
  • On the first Monday of the month I check that links still work and the prices are still right.

13 · Making it better

Three small fixes I’d suggest in the first month

None of these need new software. They work in Excel, Teams and the folders you already have.

“Identify opportunities to improve marketing processes and tools.”
1

One launch template

Every launch starts as a copy of the same tracker, checklist and folder. Wave 3 of anything takes an hour to set up, not a week.

Faster kickoffs
2

POE folder on day one

The proof folder opens on the day the campaign starts. Screenshots are filed as things go live, so claims don’t miss the cut-off.

Fewer rejected claims
3

A Friday status habit

Every Friday at noon, one page goes out: red, amber or green, one decision, what’s next. Meetings get shorter because everyone already knows.
