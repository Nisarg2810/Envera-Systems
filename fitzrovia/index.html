Rushden Station, stage by stage

Rushden Station is under construction in Danforth Village, coming Q1 2027. Here's how I'd support the team from hoarding to stabilization, with what the coordinator owns at each step.

Now → launch

Pre-construction

Build the interest list before there's anything to tour.

  • Hoarding artwork and a QR to register interest
  • A real Rushden page on fitzrovia.ca (finding #5)
  • Neighbourhood content: Danforth Village guides
  • Construction-milestone posts
KPI: interest-list sign-ups per week
~4 months out

Launch

Turn the list into first tours.

  • Renderings, floorplans, pricing on site and ILS on the same day
  • Interest list gets first access, then paid media
  • Launch event with site staff; vendor run-of-show
  • Every PO raised in Yardi before work starts
KPI: tours booked, cost per tour
Opening → ~90%

Lease-up

Fill suites without leaning on discounts longer than needed.

  • Weekly offer sheet, one source of truth
  • Lead source report: which channel signs leases
  • Move-in gifts and welcome kits
  • Ask happy movers for a review in week two
KPI: leases, cost per lease, occupancy
Stable

Stabilization

Keep residents, protect rent, feed the investor story.

  • Monthly curb appeal check (section 01)
  • Resident stories and events for social
  • Renewal touchpoints with site staff
  • Data for investor reports
KPI: rating, renewals, net effective rent

Where I wouldn't

Anything with a number or a promise

Prices, offers, investor figures and legal lines get typed from the source and checked by a person. AI can draft the sentence; it doesn't get to pick the number.

        Neighbourhood guide · Rushden Station

        Main and Danforth: a first look at the neighbourhood

        Where the East End's village feel meets two rapid-transit lines. A guide for everyone on the Rushden Station interest list.

        Ask people what they love about the Danforth and the same words come up: local, walkable, easy. Main and Danforth, where Rushden Station opens in Q1 2027, has all three, plus something most neighbourhoods don't: two ways to get downtown fast.

        Getting around

        Main Street station puts Line 2 at the corner, with trains west along Bloor and Danforth to the Yonge line and the core. A short walk south, Danforth GO is on the Lakeshore East line, with direct trains to Union Station. For anyone who commutes, that is the whole pitch in two sentences.

        Everyday life on the Danforth

        Danforth Avenue is a high street in the old sense: cafés, independent shops and restaurants, with the daily errands in walking distance. It's the kind of place where the flower stand and the coffee order become part of the routine.

        Green space

        The East End's parks and ravines are part of daily life here. Shaded paths and footbridges are close by, and so is room to slow down without leaving the city.

        The building

        Rushden Station is designed by bKL Architecture and part of The Collection by Fitzrovia. Suites run from studios to three bedrooms, with 9' ceilings, KitchenAid® appliances, Kohler fixtures and free gigabit internet in every home.
