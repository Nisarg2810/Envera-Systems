03

Rushden Station, stage by stage

Rushden Station is under construction in Danforth Village, coming Winter 2027. Here's how I'd support the team from hoarding to stabilization, with what the coordinator owns at each step.

Now → launch

Pre-construction

Build the interest list before there's anything to tour.

  • Hoarding artwork and a QR to register interest
  • A real Rushden page on fitzrovia.ca (finding #5)
  • Neighbourhood content: Danforth Village guides
  • Construction-milestone posts
KPI: interest-list sign-ups per week
~4 months out

Launch

Turn the list into first tours.

  • Renderings, floorplans, pricing on site and ILS on the same day
  • Interest list gets first access, then paid media
  • Launch event with site staff; vendor run-of-show
  • Every PO raised in Yardi before work starts
KPI: tours booked, cost per tour
Opening → ~90%

Lease-up

Fill suites without leaning on discounts longer than needed.

  • Weekly offer sheet, one source of truth
  • Lead source report: which channel signs leases
  • Move-in gifts and welcome kits
  • Ask happy movers for a review in week two
KPI: leases, cost per lease, occupancy
Stable

Stabilization

Keep residents, protect rent, feed the investor story.

  • Monthly curb appeal check (section 01)
  • Resident stories and events for social
  • Renewal touchpoints with site staff
  • Data for investor reports
KPI: rating, renewals, net effective rent

Three ideas Rushden could own

Hoarding

The Danforth Wall

The hoarding becomes a rotating gallery of Danforth Village spots, each with a QR that joins the interest list. It turns a construction fence into the building's first marketing channel.

Partnerships

Interest list = front row

Fitzrovia is the official rental housing partner of the Blue Jays and Raptors. A draw for game tickets among early registrants gives people a reason to sign up a year before move-in.

Move-in

A local welcome

Move-in gifts sourced from Danforth businesses. The gift is the first resident story, and those businesses usually share it.

07

AI that saves hours, with a human on every send

The job post asks for someone who keeps up with AI tools. Here's where I'd use them, and where I wouldn't.

Weekly

Listing consistency check

Here are this week's offer sheet and the text of
our ILS listing, property site and Google post for
{building}. List every place where price, offer,
dates or phone number differ from the offer sheet.
Quote the exact text. Don't rewrite anything.

The audit in section 01, done every week instead of every quarter.

Daily · review replies

Drafts, never auto-posts

"Elevator was out for two days and nobody told us."
Thank you for flagging this, and we're sorry for the trouble. Two days without an update isn't the standard we hold ourselves to. Our team would like to hear more: please reach us at the leasing office so we can make it right.
Brand voice ✓Site manager approves before posting
Per launch

Brand-voice first drafts

Using the Fitzrovia brand guide pasted below,
write 3 ILS headlines for {building} at {stage}.
Audience: {audience}. Under 60 characters. No
words on the guide's "don't use" list.

Drafts in minutes; the brand guidelines and the VP still decide.

Where I wouldn't

Anything with a number or a promise

Prices, offers, investor figures and legal lines get typed from the source and checked by a person. AI can draft the sentence; it doesn't get to pick the number.
