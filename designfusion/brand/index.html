01 · IDEA

From first sketch to finished part.

Designfusion is the only partner a manufacturer needs between an idea and a part in hand: Siemens software to design and prove it, scanning to capture what already exists, printers to make it, and engineers who know all three. Everything in the brand points at that road.

Pillar 1Engineers, not salespeople.

We talk like the application engineer on the phone: specific, calm, useful. Every page should feel like it was written by someone who has run the software.

Pillar 2Proof over adjectives.

98% renewal, 2,000+ customers, parts in five business days. A number or a real part beats "world-class" every time.

Pillar 3One partner, idea to part.

Software, scanning, printing and services are one story. Every piece of content shows where it sits on that road.

02 · LOGO

Keep the mark. Give it room.

The wordmark and arc stay exactly as they are. Use the lime-and-white version on Ink, the indigo-and-lime version on Paper and white. Clear space is the height of the arc on every side. Minimum width: 96 px on screen, 25 mm in print.

On Paper and white

Tight headlines. Plain body. Spec labels.

Everything you read. 17 px on web, 15–16 px in email.

05 · THE SCAN LINE

One line that tells the whole story.

A single lime line splits a real part into its physical half and its digital half. It is Designfusion's signature, and it only means one thing: we work on both sides of it.

06 · PHOTOGRAPHY

Real parts, real benches.

Your own photography is the best asset you have. Shoot parts on the workbench and printers mid-job, in natural light, with the shop visible behind. Three treatments cover every use.

StraightProduct pages, case studies, blog headers.
Scan splitLaunches, scanning, ads, heroes.
Broadcast duotoneWebinars, events, anything live.
DOShow the part, the machine or the screen doing the work.
DON'TUse handshakes, generic factories or glowing "AI" robots.
