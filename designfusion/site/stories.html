Example story · format for the first scanning job

A housing nobody could buy, back on the line in nine days.

The guard housing on a 1990s filler cracked. The builder no longer existed and the only drawing was a sketch with half the dimensions missing. We scanned it on site, rebuilt it in NX, added a fillet where the crack started, merged three pieces into one and printed it in carbon-fibre-reinforced nylon.
