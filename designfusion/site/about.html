Twenty years of picking up the phone.

We started with five people and no customers. Today more than 2,000 manufacturers across North America work with us, and 98% of them renew.

Our story

Built by people who came from Siemens.

Our founders have served the Siemens PLM community for more than thirty years, first inside Siemens and then at Designfusion. Customers work under deadline pressure, so we became the first Siemens PLM partner in North America to offer a 1-800 support line.

Since then we have added 3D printing, post-processing, scanning and engineering services, so a customer can go from first sketch to finished part without changing partners. We still measure ourselves the same way: by whether you renew.
