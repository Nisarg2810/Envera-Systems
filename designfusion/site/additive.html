Markforged

Strong enough to replace metal.

Markforged composite printers lay continuous carbon fibre inside Onyx, a tough nylon blend. The result is jigs, fixtures, jaws and end-use parts that are ready the same day.

  • FX20 and FX10 industrial composite printers
  • X7 carbon fibre and Metal X for metal parts
  • Mark Two and Onyx Pro desktop printers
  • Simulation and inspection software for every print

Formlabs

Fast, fine detail. Real materials.

Formlabs SLA and SLS printers bring prototyping and short runs in-house, so you iterate in hours instead of waiting on a vendor.

  • Form 4 and Form 4L resin printers
  • Fuse 1+ and Fuse X1 industrial SLS
  • Form Studio 1 for production workflows
  • Prototypes, tooling and end-use parts

Post-processing

Parts that look finished, because they are.

Printing is half the job. AMT PostPro systems depowder, blast and vapour smooth parts so they come off the line ready to use.

  • PostPro vapour smoothing, including polypropylene
  • Standard, advanced and continuous depowdering
  • Surface and basket blasting
