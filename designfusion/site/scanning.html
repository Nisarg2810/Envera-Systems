How it works

You decide how far we go.

Scan

At your facility or ours. The scanner is handheld, so we can come to the part when the part cannot come to us.

Model

Our engineers rebuild the scan as a parametric model in NX or Solid Edge, ready for drawings and manufacturing.

Improve

Optional. Lighter, stronger or in fewer pieces, using Design for Additive Manufacturing.

Make

Printed on Markforged or Formlabs, through our parts brokerage, or machined in your own shop.

Questions we hear

Not on the list? Ask an engineer →

Can you scan it at our facility?

Yes. Give us time on site, or ship the parts to us. Large or fixed parts are usually easier to scan where they are.

What do we get back?

It depends on the package: scan data, a parametric CAD model, or a redesigned part ready to print. Models come as native NX or Solid Edge files, or neutral formats like STEP.

How accurate is it?

Accurate enough to reverse-engineer and inspect production parts. Tell us the tolerance you need and we will confirm it before we start.

Do we need to buy equipment?

No. This is a service. If you want to bring scanning or printing in-house later, we can talk about that too.

How long does it take?

That depends on the size of the part, its detail, and how much modelling you need. You get a timeline with your quote.
