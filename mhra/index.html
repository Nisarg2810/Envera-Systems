Your job description, my first 90 days

You wrote the list. Here's how I'd run it.

01
Creative meetings

Lead bi-weekly MHRA creative meetings

A one-page agenda sent 24 hours before: last two weeks' numbers, three ideas per brand, one decision needed. Every meeting ends with owners and dates.

02
Content plans + calendars

Monthly calendars for all three brands

Each brand gets its own pillars, tone and recurring formats, built from the audit, not assumptions. Calendars live in Asana, reviewed before the month starts.

03
Shoots

Plan and lead organic content shoots

Shot lists that squeeze several usable pieces out of every shoot: hero photos, Reels, TikToks, behind-the-scenes and Stories, not just one post.

04
Reporting

Weekly KPI scoreboard + monthly deck

Reach, engagement, saves, follower growth and watch time, live from week one. The monthly deck says what worked, why, what didn't, and what's next.

05
Boosting

Recommend boosts, manage weekly budgets

Paid support only goes behind organic posts with proven signals (saves, shares, watch time). No guesswork.

06
Influencers + community

Influencer strategy and a daily community pulse

Creators picked for genuine audience fit. Comments and DMs double as research: what people ask for feeds menu, promo and content ideas.
