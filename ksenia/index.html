Team campaign · ElevraaCybersecurity · ABM · 3 months

4 sales calls booked from 50 target accounts, plus 7 referral partnerships and $15,000+ in active pipeline, for a cybersecurity firm that had never closed a client.

Goal
Brahm Security launched in May 2025 with real expertise and no market presence. No clients, no audience, no pipeline. The goal was its first qualified sales conversations.
What we did
  • Built the founder's thought leadership on LinkedIn first, so buyers saw his thinking before any outreach.
  • Picked 50 Bangalore software companies that ship code continuously, the buyers who feel the pain of slow security testing most.
  • Ran a multi-touch sequence per account: a useful resource to decision makers, content about that company's month, connecting with every decision maker, a follow-up, then a custom landing page.
  • In parallel, set up referral partnerships with providers who serve the same buyers.
Outcome
4 sales-qualified calls, 7 referral partnerships and $15,000+ in active pipeline, in about 3 months.
One example
The order mattered most. Content went first, partnerships next, outreach last. By the time a prospect got a message, they had already seen the founder's thinking, so none of the 4 calls was a cold one.
Team campaign · ElevraaAgency founders · Direct mail · 1 account

One package, one client. Three agency co-founders who had ignored months of templated outreach took the meeting and signed.

Goal
Get a meeting with three founders of a marketing agency who were ignoring every cold message they got.
What we did
  • Read years of their public posts and comments, going back to 2019, when almost nobody was reading them.
  • Turned it into a printed comic book: their college photos redrawn as illustrations and the founding story rebuilt chapter by chapter, with one story for each founder.
  • Closed the book with a section on where the agency could go next.
Outcome
A meeting, then a signed client.
One example
In the meeting, one founder stopped mid-sentence and asked how we knew something the three of them had only discussed privately. Nothing had leaked. It came from piecing together years of public signals.
