Client campaignCybersecurity · ABM + direct mail · 3 months

4 sales calls booked from 45 target accounts, plus 7 referral partnerships and $15,000+ in active pipeline, for a cybersecurity firm that had never closed a client.

Goal
A new cybersecurity firm with real expertise and nothing else: no clients, no audience, no pipeline. The goal was its first qualified sales conversations.
What we did
  • Picked 45 Bangalore companies that had just raised seed or Series A. They are about to scale, so security is about to matter.
  • Sat with the firm's security expert and sales expert to work out what each company actually needed.
  • Wrote a proper report for every company: how to handle security in its next stage, step by step. Built a landing page for each one too.
  • A month before the mail went out, the founder's LinkedIn started connecting with each company's decision makers, with content tuned to that niche only.
  • On the day, the mailer below landed. In parallel, we set up referral partnerships with providers serving the same buyers.
Outcome
4 sales-qualified calls, 7 referral partnerships and $15,000+ in active pipeline, in about 3 months.
One example
The envelope. It said CONFIDENTIAL in big letters, with the company's own logo at the bottom. On the back, a seal shaped like a broken lock, and under it: "Some confidential things are already broken." Inside, a card with a QR code that opened that company's own page, and its report.

Client campaignAgency founders · Direct mail · 1 account

One package, one client. Three agency co-founders who had ignored months of templated outreach took the meeting and signed.

Goal
Get a meeting with three founders of a marketing agency who were ignoring every cold message they got.
What we did
  • Read years of their public posts and comments, going back to 2019, when almost nobody was reading them.
  • Turned it into a printed comic book: their college photos redrawn as illustrations and the founding story rebuilt chapter by chapter, with one story for each founder.
  • Closed the book with a section on where the agency could go next.
Outcome
A meeting, then a signed client.
One example
In the meeting, one founder stopped mid-sentence and asked how we knew something the three of them had only discussed privately. Nothing had leaked. It came from piecing together years of public signals.
